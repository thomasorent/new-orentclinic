# Orent Clinic UI redesign proposals

These are two static, responsive design directions based on the current homepage. They intentionally leave the production React app unchanged so a direction can be selected before implementation.

## Current-page findings

- The page contains the right core information, but every section has similar visual weight, so there is no clear path from first visit to calling or messaging the clinic.
- The unrelated landscape hero weakens trust and does not communicate orthopedic or ENT care.
- The long temporary-closure notice dominates the first screen and competes with navigation.
- Appointment actions are commented out while phone and WhatsApp details appear much later on the page.
- Doctor credentials are valuable trust signals, but their cards feel secondary and the image treatment is inconsistent.
- Emoji-based service icons and the default table treatment make the experience feel less distinctive than the clinical expertise deserves.
- The automatic chat welcome panel obscures the hero on first load.
- The visual system mixes blue, green, purple link defaults, deep shadows, and generic browser styles.

## Concept A — Calm, human, local

Preview: `concept-a.html`

A warm editorial direction built around the two doctors, their shared practice, and reassuring access to care. The design uses cream, ink blue, terracotta, and sage, with generous whitespace and prominent call/WhatsApp actions.

Best for: patients and families who value trust, continuity, and a personal local-clinic experience.

Key ideas:

- Put the doctors and their specialties in the hero instead of a generic landscape.
- Compress the closure message into a clear status card.
- Present the patient journey as three simple steps.
- Use a quiet, premium visual system that feels warm rather than corporate.

## Concept B — Clear, fast, clinical

Preview: `concept-b.html`

A crisp service-led direction optimized for fast scanning. It makes hours, fees, phone access, departments, and directions immediately visible through a modular grid.

Best for: mobile visitors arriving with a specific need who want to decide and act quickly.

Key ideas:

- Make the next action obvious within seconds.
- Move practical details above the fold.
- Use structured service pathways for Orthopedics and ENT.
- Keep the visual language modern and clinical without feeling sterile.

## Recommendation

Use **Concept A** as the brand foundation, then borrow Concept B's compact hours/fees and action bar. This combination gives Orent Clinic a distinctive, trusted presence while still serving urgent, task-focused visitors.
